Training Hub Pre-pilot

Repo: lms-discovery. Brightspace stays the system of record for courses, enrollment and progress; the Hub handles discovery, browsing, search and handoff links.

Two deployments

SiteWhoHow login works
Team siteMLRI staffLogin required on every page (Brightspace OAuth). Staff can switch between demo personas after signing in.
Demo siteStakeholders, advisory committeeNever asks for login. A view picker offers new learner, returning learner and supervisor views. Always "structure only". Builds fail if demo mode is switched on anywhere else.

What is real and what is sample data

  • Real: Brightspace sign-in, signed session cookie, token refresh, route gating, admin routes guarded by a secret.
  • Sample data: the course catalog (hard-coded) and dashboards (mock service). The swap points are the Supabase learning_items table and /api/me/dashboard.
  • Browser only: progress, saved items, notifications and profile are stored in the learner's browser for now.
  • Defaults: real logins get the most restricted user type until the UPL access matrix drives role mapping.

Curriculum shown in the Hub

Two branches: Legal Skills (first priority) and Substantive Law (shown as planned structure). Eight skill areas: Legal Research, Legal Writing, Pretrial, Trial, Appellate Practice, ADR, Legislative Advocacy, Ethics. Built offerings include Welcome to the Training Hub, Faculty Handbook: Interactive Elements, Eviction Defense: The First 48 Hours, the eviction-intake practice scenario, the Hearsay prototype and the Pastry Station practice course (team site only, a made-up course built the way an attorney course will be).

Brightspace Manager In Progress

Repo: brightspace-manager. Internal staff app with two modes. Rule: it is the only thing that writes to Brightspace or to Supabase catalog data.

  • Operator mode: dashboard, Course Inventory, Sync Diagnostics (preview, confirm, upsert, audit), Brightspace Files (read-only), Supabase data inspector, settings. Evaluation and Learner Progress pages use mock data. Integrity Checker is still a placeholder.
  • Author mode: Course Studio (three-pane builder with ZIP export), shared Curriculum Map, author guides, Hub listing flow. Faculty and My Courses are "coming soon".
  • Sign-in: invite-only. Google restricted to mlri.org, with email codes as a break-glass option.
  • Brightspace access: still uses a person's OAuth refresh-token chain. The service-user (client credentials) flow is the next integration milestone, and the stored-token approach does not work on serverless hosting.

Pretrial & Trial Skills Research Planning

Repo: pretrial-trial-skills (created Oct 21, 2026). Working home for the cognitive task analysis (CTA) research, curriculum planning and the resulting LMS content. No LMS content or configuration has changed yet.

  • Approach: Four-Component Instructional Design (4C/ID): whole tasks, task classes from easy to hard, support that fades, supportive vs procedural information, and part-task practice. One hypothetical case file is carried through every stage.
  • Structure: LACE curriculum → Legal Skills → Program 1: Pretrial and Trial Skills (top-level whole task: the lifecycle of a trial). Program 2: Legal Research is a later cycle.
  • Decisions: 20 logged. Only DEC-001 ("Program" as the umbrella name) is resolved. Five are proposed and DEC-009 (December to March milestones) is tentative. The rest are open, including DEC-015 program scope (does it start at trial prep, filing or client intake; end at verdict, post-trial motions or appeal?) and DEC-020 sequencing (Ashley is drafting).
  • Glossary: 29 working terms, almost all proposed. The "Agreed definitions" section is still empty.

Native Apps Plan Planned

Source: lms-discovery/docs/planning/native-apps-plan.md (Nov 25, 2026). Goal: an unlisted iPhone and Android app by April/May 2027.

  • Approach: a thin app shell around the Training Hub. Brightspace opens inside the app (no Safari sheet), with a native "Back to Hub" bar, deep links to course content and a clean return to where the learner came from.
  • Biggest risk: sign-in inside the app. Google blocks embedded sign-in and Microsoft discourages it. Needs an answer in December.
  • Other risks: deep links may skip completion tracking; Apple rejects apps that are only a website, so the plan adds push reminders, offline saved items and a native tab bar.
  • Start now: Apple Developer Program and Google Play Console as an organization (Apple needs a D-U-N-S number), an App Store reviewer account and a privacy policy URL.

Before the Pilot

From the Training Hub's production-readiness list (Nov 16, 2026). Tracked in lms-discovery/docs/production-readiness.md.

Must do before any real learner signs in

  • Turn off demo mode and demo personas in production.
  • Make Brightspace token refresh safe when several requests refresh at once.
  • Retire the Google demo login, or record why it stays.
  • Real role mapping driven by the UPL access matrix.
  • Real catalog and dashboard data in place of sample data.
  • Error monitoring.
  • Branch protection on the main branch.
  • Production secrets and a rotation note for the session secret.

Should do before the pilot

  • No names in team URLs.
  • Supabase migrations as the single source of truth, with explicit grants (Supabase changed its defaults on Oct 30, 2026).
  • Database access-rule (RLS) tests run automatically.
  • Durable rate limiting and a stricter content security policy.
  • Manual screen-reader pass.
  • Privacy notice covering analytics and browser storage.
